KESTREL BANK - TEST CREDENTIALS (fictional, simulated environment)
====================================================================
Web app : https://mohammedghouse86.github.io/Bankingapp/
API     : https://bankingapp-kpp3.onrender.com   (docs: /docs)

Tenants are bank branches. Managers, tellers and customers of one tenant
cannot see or act on another tenant's customers, accounts, transactions,
approvals, staff or reports (out-of-tenant IDs return 404).

Notes
- Critical actions (role/password changes, network/IP settings, contact
  changes, PII export, data reset) also require the X-Confirm-Password
  header containing the caller's own password.
- Data is in memory: a server restart or POST /api/v1/system/reset
  restores everything, including any changed passwords.
